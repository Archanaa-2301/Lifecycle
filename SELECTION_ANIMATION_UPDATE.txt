LIFEX SELECTION ANIMATION UPDATE

This update strengthens selection feedback throughout the existing LIFEX workflow.

Shared interaction:
- A selected card receives a strong blue commit highlight.
- A branded selection token travels from the clicked card along a bright curved path.
- The destination hierarchy / selected panel receives a landing pulse.
- Selection is committed only after the animation finishes.
- Reduced-motion preferences are respected.

Updated selection points:
1. OEM cards: click Toyota, Ford, BMW, Honda or Tesla directly. The selected OEM travels toward a hierarchy destination before the vehicle page opens.
2. Project vehicle cards: no model is preselected. Selecting a vehicle animates it into the OEM-to-vehicle hierarchy. Continue to overview is enabled after the landing.
3. Vehicle Overview: hotspot markers and system-list cards animate the selected system toward the active-system panel; Rear Suspension proceeds to component hierarchy after the transition.
4. Rear Suspension Component Hierarchy: component cards animate into the selected-component footer before becoming active.
5. Component Workspace: repository rows and Engineering Intelligence actions animate toward the selection hierarchy before opening.
6. Global Repository: Design Rules / Templates / Process, vehicle-system cards and component cards use the same selection flight.
7. Knowledge Repository, Manual Entry workflows, Internal Parts and legacy vehicle / subsystem selection pages also use the shared animation.
